### 4.B. Rent Ordinance Protected Tenancy — Unit 2

**SELLER DISCLOSURE:**

> *"Raymond T. Castellanos has occupied Unit 2 continuously since March 2003. His tenancy commenced prior to January 1, 2005, and the unit is subject to rent stabilization under San Francisco Administrative Code Chapter 37 ('Rent Ordinance'). Buyer takes title subject to this tenancy."*

**NOTICE TO BUYER:** Under the San Francisco Rent Ordinance, just cause is required to terminate a protected tenancy. Permitted just causes for eviction include, but are not limited to, owner move-in (§ 37.9(a)(8)), Ellis Act withdrawal (§ 37.9(a)(13)), and substantial rehabilitation (§ 37.9(a)(11)). An owner move-in eviction of a tenant in continuous occupancy for 10 or more years requires payment of relocation assistance currently set at $7,942.00 per eligible occupant plus $3,971.00 per additional occupant. Tenant Castellanos has occupied the unit for 23+ years. Buyer is advised to consult with counsel experienced in San Francisco landlord-tenant law prior to Close of Escrow. Seller makes no representation regarding Buyer's ability to recover possession of Unit 2.

**BUYER'S COUNSEL NOTE (for file — not part of executed agreement):** *10-day inspection contingency is materially shorter than the market standard of 17 calendar days (C.A.R. Form RPA default) or the 21 days frequently negotiated for multi-unit income property. Given (a) the unpermitted addition, (b) the active sewer easement encroachment, (c) the 2009 deed of trust of uncertain successor interest, and (d) the long-term protected tenancy, this timeline is aggressive. Recommend seeking extension to minimum 17 days or negotiating a carve-out allowing extended review of the DBI permit record.*
